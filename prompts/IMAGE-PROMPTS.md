# Image prompts for ChatGPT (image generation)

Generate each image, then save it into `assets/img/` with the exact filename shown. Export as WebP (or JPG and rename the extension in the page if you prefer), about 1600px on the long side, under 300 KB. The site shows its built-in artwork until each file exists, so you can add them one at a time.

Shared style line (paste at the end of every prompt):
> Photorealistic, editorial magazine quality, cinematic lighting, deep navy and warm brass-gold color palette, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no people's faces.

## 1. `hero-skyline.webp` (portrait, 4:5, 1600x2000)
A financial-district skyline at blue hour, shot from a low angle looking up between glass towers, windows glowing warm gold against a deep navy sky, soft haze near the horizon, large calm negative space in the upper third. Premium, quiet, confident mood. Vertical composition.

## 2. `hero-detail.webp` (square, 1:1, 1200x1200)
Close-up still life on a dark walnut desk: a leather-bound law book, a brass fountain pen and a folded navy pocket square, lit by a single warm desk lamp, deep navy shadows, crisp focus on the pen nib. Square composition.

## 3. `project-rooted-grounds.webp` (landscape, 16:10, 1600x1000)
Moody flat lay of a ceramic coffee cup on a dark navy linen surface, a notebook with a pencil, coffee beans scattered, warm brass tones, overhead angle, space on the left for a number overlay.

## 4. `project-deal.webp` (landscape, 16:10, 1600x1000)
Abstract architectural photograph of two glass skyscrapers whose reflections meet, viewed from below at dusk, navy glass with gold reflections, a feeling of two companies joining. Clean geometric lines.

## 5. `project-primacy.webp` (landscape, 16:10, 1600x1000)
A quiet university library reading table with a stack of open books and a green banker's lamp turned to warm gold, navy shadows, bookshelves softly blurred behind. Avoid cliches such as gavels, scales of justice and columns.

## 6. `project-caremark.webp` (landscape, 16:10, 1600x1000)
An empty, modern boardroom at night: a long dark wood table, leather chairs, city lights glowing gold through floor-to-ceiling windows, deep navy tones. No people.

## 7. `headshot.jpg` (portrait, 4:5, 1200x1500)
Use a real photo of yourself rather than a generated one. Ask a friend to shoot it near a window in soft daylight: navy blazer, plain light background, shoulders angled slightly, a relaxed confident smile, eye level. Crop to 4:5. If you want an AI retouch, upload your own photo to ChatGPT and ask: "Keep my face exactly as it is. Only improve lighting and background: soft neutral studio backdrop, natural skin, no beautification."

## Optional
`og-image.jpg` (1200x630): hero-skyline cropped wide with space on the left, used when the site link is shared.
