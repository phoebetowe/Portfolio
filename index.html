About

Why corporate law

Corporate law is where finance and moral reasoning meet. My finance coursework taught me how companies are valued, financed, and structured. My Philosophy & Law minor taught me to ask what directors, shareholders, and institutions owe one another, and how to build a careful argument when the answer is contested.

Outside the classroom I've led philanthropy for a 200+ member chapter, co-founded a student organization focused on applied AI, and spent summers working 15-hour days on a Montana ranch. Each of those taught me something about judgment, accountability, and following through when the work is hard. I'm now preparing for the LSAT and law school, with the goal of advising businesses through transactions and governance decisions.
